# Phase 3 Ideas Backlog

Scope-creep candidates that come up during Phase 2 (Project Bane) work but
aren't required for the current milestone's exit criteria go here instead of
being built early, per the Phase 2 working agreements.

After the 2026-07-15 Phase 2 scope revision, the Phase 1 first-contract
route shape is part of Phase 2 parity. A larger Contract Run
meta-progression layer - additional contracts, a broader procedural map,
expanded route generation, or campaign-scale structure - remains Phase 3+
scope and belongs here once the revised Phase 2 Rogue adventure is done.

## Format

Each entry: what it is, which milestone surfaced it, why it was deferred.

---

## Additional Playable Classes Beyond Rogue

Surfaced during `P2:R1:T7`.

Mage, Crusader, and any other future classes remain Phase 3+ because revised
Phase 2 is focused on the first proven Rogue Adventure. In Phase 2 they may
remain visible as disabled future options, but should not receive playable
class data, talent trees, balance work, or route-specific content.

## Additional Contracts Beyond The Gilded Serpent

Surfaced during `P2:R1:T7`.

The Gilded Serpent first contract route is Phase 2 parity. Additional
contracts would expand content breadth beyond the validated first Rogue run and
should wait until the Phase 2 Adventure can be played, tested, saved, and
exported.

## Broader Procedural Contract Run Meta-Progression

Surfaced during `P2:R1:T7`.

Phase 2 should implement the authored Phase 1 first-contract route, not a
general procedural map, expanded route-generation system, campaign-scale
structure, or meta-progression layer. Those systems belong after the narrow
Rogue Adventure proves the Godot dashboard loop.

## Full Training Room Mode

Surfaced during `P2:R1:T7`.

The full Phase 1 Training Room is deferred because the revised Phase 2
definition of done is a complete downloadable Rogue Adventure, not every
support mode from the prototype. A later Training Room should share the same
combat/build systems while providing freeform controls for active trees,
passives, gear quality and affixes, Legendary weapon choice, rotation, target,
combat duration, seed, and practice gold.

Update from 2026-07-17: `P2:R8` may include a compact Training Room Lite if
the Rogue Adventure export is already stable and the work stays low-risk. That
late playtest-support version does not replace this Phase 3 backlog item for
the full freeform Training Room mode.

## Training Room Target Set

Surfaced during `P2:R1:T7`.

Training Dummy, Armored Guard, and Venom-Resistant Slime should be seeded as
player-facing practice targets when Training Room returns. Placeholder/test
monsters may remain in Phase 2 tests, but these targets are not needed in the
Adventure path.

## Full Art, Animation, And Polish Production

Surfaced during `P2:R1:T7`.

`P2:R7` should improve readability, hierarchy, interaction clarity, and
playtest presentation, but a complete art/animation/juice pass belongs after
the Phase 2 Adventure is proven.

## Additional Platform Export Breadth

Surfaced during `P2:R1:T7`.

Windows desktop remains the minimum Phase 2 playtest export target. Web or
additional platform exports are Phase 3+ unless they prove low-effort during
`P2:R8` and do not delay the shareable Rogue Adventure build.
